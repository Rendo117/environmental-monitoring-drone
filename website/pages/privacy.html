Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. When you open a page, your browser sends your IP address and technical request data (for example the page requested, the time and the browser type) to GitHub's servers. Legal basis: legitimate interest in publishing and securely hosting the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub may process data in the United States and relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses for such transfers. Details are in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Storage in your browser

If you use the “Toggle dark mode” button, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the key theme, so the next page opens in the same mode. It never leaves your device. You can delete it by clearing site data in your browser. This storage is strictly necessary to provide a feature you request, so no consent banner is needed.
