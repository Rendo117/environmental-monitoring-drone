Credits and sources

  • Quadcopter model after T. Luukkonen, Modelling and control of quadcopter, Aalto University, 2011.
  • Geiger counter topology after the “homebrew Geiger counter circuit” published by the element14 community.
  • MQ-2 sensitivity curves from the Hanwei Electronics MQ-2 datasheet. MPU-6050 register settings from the InvenSense product specification.
  • The flight-controller structure (set-point calculation, mixer, battery compensation) follows the open-source YMFC-AL project by Joop Brokking and its derivative by lobodol, adapted in the thesis.
