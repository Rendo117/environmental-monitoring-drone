Environmental monitoring drone for emergency situations

A quadcopter that flies a gas sensor and a Geiger–Müller counter into an area people should not enter. The operator steers it over the internet and reads the measurements in real time.

How it works

The design keeps the internet out of the stabilisation loop. A small microcontroller keeps the aircraft level 250 times a second. A Raspberry Pi on board relays the operator's commands and the measurements through a Firebase real-time database, so the operator can be anywhere with a connection.

What the 2026 revision adds

The original thesis was reviewed line by line. The firmware now compiles with every sensor enabled, arms only on the stick sequence, and has a fail-safe. A sign error that would have flipped the aircraft on pitch is fixed. The Java and Python links are authenticated, and the mathematical model now covers the full Newton–Euler dynamics, with a simulation that checks the controller against them.

  • 52° / 10.6 dBphase and gain margin of the roll-rate loop with the thesis PID gains
  • 0.6 stime for the 2022 pitch sign error to tip the simulated aircraft past 70°
  • 18 + 7 + 7firmware, Raspberry Pi and Java defects corrected (see errata)
  • 1.2 m/ssimulated fail-safe touchdown speed with a barometer, against 11–20 m/s without

Rezumat

Proiectul este un sistem pentru măsurarea concentrației de gaze periculoase și a nivelului radiațiilor ionizante cu ajutorul unei drone de tip quadcopter, comandată prin internet. Sistemul are trei componente: o aplicație de control și monitorizare scrisă în Java, o bază de date Firebase și un calculator de bord Raspberry Pi 4 care comunică cu microcontrolerul ATmega328P al dronei și cu traductoarele (MQ-2, tub Geiger–Müller, MPU-6050). Ediția revizuită corectează erorile din lucrarea originală, completează codul sursă și extinde modelul matematic cu o analiză de stabilitate și o simulare.
