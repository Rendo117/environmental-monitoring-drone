Safety and regulations

This is general information, not legal advice. Rules change, so check with the authority before flying.

Aviation rules (EU / Romania)

  • Drone operations in the EU fall under Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/947 and Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/945. In Romania the competent authority is the Romanian Civil Aeronautical Authority (AACR). National provisions are in the Air Code (Law 21/2020).
  • The aircraft weighs ~1.2 kg and has no class marking, so it is a privately built UAS. In the open category it may fly only in subcategory A3: far from people and at least 150 m from residential, commercial, industrial or recreational areas, below 120 m, and in visual line of sight (VLOS).
  • The operator must register with AACR (drones > 250 g) and display the operator ID on the aircraft. The remote pilot needs the A1/A3 online training and test.
  • Controlling the drone "from anywhere in the world over the internet" (the thesis concept) is a beyond-visual-line-of-sight (BVLOS) operation. That is the specific category, which requires an AACR operational authorisation based on a risk assessment (SORA) or a standard scenario.
  • Check the geographical zones (restricted/prohibited areas, airports, military sites) before every flight. Emergency sites are often under temporary flight restrictions, so coordinate with the incident commander.

Radio

The aircraft uses the Raspberry Pi's Wi-Fi or a 4G modem only (licence-free / operator networks). Do not add transmitters without checking ANCOM rules.

Hazards specific to this project

  • High voltage: the Geiger board generates ~400 V. Capacitors stay charged after power-off.
  • LiPo batteries: fire risk. Charge in a LiPo bag, never unattended, and store at 3.8 V/cell.
  • Propellers: remove them for all bench work.
  • Radioactive check sources: use only exempt sources (e.g. a lantern mantle or a small calibration source) and follow their handling instructions. Do not open sealed sources.
  • Measurement limits: the MQ-2 and the Geiger counter here are indicative, uncalibrated instruments. Do not use their readings for life-safety decisions. Use certified instruments and qualified personnel.

Data protection

The aircraft records gas, radiation, attitude and battery data, with no camera and no personal data. If you add a camera, GDPR applies to images of people.
